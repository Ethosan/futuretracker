Trade Journal

Get started

A futures trading journal for MES, MNQ, MGC and XAUUSD. Log each trade with what you felt and what you did wrong, then see where the money goes. Live and Prop accounts are tracked separately.

Free, and no new password. Your trades go to a private sheet in your own Google Drive. The journal can only open files it made. Privacy

Connect a Google Sheet

Do this once per device. The journal only talks to your sheet, through a small script you own. Keep the web app URL private: anyone who has it can read and change your trades.
