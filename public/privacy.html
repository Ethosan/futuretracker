Trade Journal: privacy

Trade Journal is a personal project by Ethan Tam. It has no server and no database of its own. It doesn't use analytics or ads.

Where your trades go

  • Sign in with Google: trades are saved to a spreadsheet called "Trade Journal" in your own Google Drive. The app asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and change files it created and nothing else in your Drive.
  • Your own Apps Script: trades go to your sheet through a script you deploy yourself.
  • This device: trades stay in your browser's local storage.
  • Demo: sample data in memory. Nothing is saved.

Google data

Your Google access token is kept in this browser tab only and expires after about an hour. Data from Google is shown to you in the app and is not sent anywhere else, sold, or shared. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To remove access, open Settings, then Reset, or go to your Google account permissions. Your sheet stays in your Drive until you delete it.
